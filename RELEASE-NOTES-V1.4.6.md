# Accounts V1.4.6 — Controlled Booking PDF Import

This cumulative release restores the manual invoice workflow for invoices generated by BookingSystem2026.

## What changed

- New Invoice can read the current BookingSystem2026 PDF layout locally, without Gemini or any external AI service.
- The form is populated with the printed invoice number, issue date, couple, contact details, wedding date, due date, total, paid amount, outstanding balance, package, add-ons and full package wording.
- The uploaded invoice is only previewed at first. Nothing is saved until **Import reviewed invoice** is pressed.
- Every extracted field remains visible and editable before import.
- The original PDF and its printed WBM number are retained unchanged in Accounts.
- Duplicate invoice numbers and mismatched PDF/form numbers are blocked.
- Imported source PDFs are preserved if the Accounts metadata is edited later.
- Automatic Booking integration remains optional and can stay disabled.
